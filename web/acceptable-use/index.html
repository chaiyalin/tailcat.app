可接受使用政策 / Acceptable Use Policy

tailcat.app 只应用于合法、自愿、低风险的一对一传输。请尊重他人、网络和公共中继资源。

tailcat.app is for lawful, consensual, low-risk one-to-one transfers. Respect other people, networks, and shared relay capacity.

中文

1. 适用范围

本政策适用于访问 tailcat.app、创建或加入房间，以及通过其发送、接收或展示的全部内容和流量。使用服务即表示你同时接受公开测试条款。

2. 禁止内容

不得使用服务发送、索取、协助获取或传播：

  • 任何违法内容，或以违法方式获得的内容；
  • 儿童性虐待材料、性剥削内容，或任何伤害未成年人的材料；
  • 未经同意的私密影像、跟踪骚扰、威胁、敲诈、仇恨或针对性虐待内容；
  • 侵犯版权、商标、商业秘密、隐私、肖像权或其他权利的内容；
  • 恶意软件、勒索软件、窃密工具、钓鱼材料、被盗凭据或用于未授权访问的数据；
  • 受出口管制、制裁或其他适用限制而不得传输的内容。

3. 禁止行为

  • 未经授权访问、扫描、攻击、干扰或破坏设备、账户、网络、DERP、中继或本站；
  • 利用漏洞、逃避安全控制、伪造身份、欺骗收件人或诱导其运行有害文件；
  • 自动化批量传输、压力测试、挖矿、代理转售、商业中继或任何消耗不成比例资源的用途；
  • 绕过文件大小、速率、并发、浏览器兼容或其他技术限制；
  • 把服务用于紧急通信、生命安全、医疗诊断、关键基础设施控制、受监管记录归档或其他失败会造成严重伤害的高风险场景；
  • 帮助他人实施上述行为，或反复连接不愿与你通信的人。

4. 文件与安全责任

本站不查看、审核或病毒扫描端到端加密内容。加密不代表文件安全或发送者可信。发送者必须拥有合法权限；接收者必须在确认来源后主动同意保存，并自行使用本地安全工具检查。

5. 公平使用公共资源

公共 Tailcat DERP 限速且无 SLA。不得持续占用连接、拆分文件逃避限制、制造无效流量或影响其他用户。单文件、消息、语音时长和并发限制属于服务边界，并可能在测试期间调整。

6. 举报与处理

举报时请提供可验证的时间、行为描述、受影响方和必要的非敏感技术信息。由于服务无账号、无内容数据库且内容端到端加密，我们可能无法读取内容、识别用户或恢复历史。我们仍可在技术上可行和适当时限制访问、关闭功能、保留必要证据或回应有效法律要求。

如有人面临紧迫危险，请联系当地紧急服务；不要依赖本项目处理紧急事件。

7. 善意安全研究

未经许可不得对生产服务进行自动扫描、负载或破坏性测试。善意研究应最小化数据访问和影响，停止发现后的进一步利用，并按安全说明报告。此政策本身不构成对违反法律或第三方权利行为的授权。

English

1. Scope

This policy applies when you access tailcat.app, create or join a room, or send, receive, or display any content or traffic through it. Use also means acceptance of the Public Beta Terms.

2. Prohibited content

Do not use the service to send, request, facilitate access to, or distribute:

  • Anything illegal, or content obtained through unlawful means;
  • Child sexual abuse material, sexual exploitation, or any material that harms minors;
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery, stalking, harassment, threats, extortion, hateful abuse, or targeted abuse;
  • Material that infringes copyright, trademark, trade-secret, privacy, publicity, or other rights;
  • Malware, ransomware, credential stealers, phishing material, stolen credentials, or data intended for unauthorized access; or
  • Material whose transfer is prohibited by export controls, sanctions, or other applicable restrictions.

3. Prohibited conduct

  • Accessing, scanning, attacking, disrupting, or damaging any device, account, network, DERP relay, or this site without authorization;
  • Exploiting vulnerabilities, evading security controls, impersonating others, deceiving recipients, or inducing them to run harmful files;
  • Automated bulk transfers, stress testing, mining, proxy resale, commercial relaying, or any use that consumes disproportionate resources;
  • Circumventing file-size, rate, concurrency, browser-support, or other technical limits;
  • Using the service for emergency communications, life safety, medical diagnosis, critical infrastructure control, regulated records retention, or another high-risk purpose where failure could cause serious harm; or
  • Helping others do any of the above, or repeatedly connecting to someone who does not want to communicate with you.

4. File and security responsibility

The site does not inspect, moderate, or malware-scan end-to-end encrypted content. Encryption does not make a file safe or a sender trustworthy. Senders must have lawful authority; recipients must explicitly approve saving a file after confirming its source and must inspect it with local security tools.

5. Fair use of shared resources

Public Tailcat DERP relays are rate-limited and have no SLA. Do not hold connections open unnecessarily, split files to evade limits, generate useless traffic, or impair other users. File, message, voice-duration, and concurrency limits are service boundaries and may change during beta.

6. Reports and response

A report should include a verifiable time, description, affected party, and only the non-sensitive technical information needed. Because the service has no accounts or content database and content is end-to-end encrypted, we may be unable to read content, identify a user, or recover history. Where technically feasible and appropriate, we may still restrict access, disable features, preserve necessary evidence, or respond to valid legal process.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services; do not rely on this project for emergency response.

7. Good-faith security research

Do not perform automated scans, load tests, or destructive testing against production without permission. Good-faith research must minimize data access and impact, stop further exploitation after discovery, and follow the Security Notes for reporting. This policy does not itself authorize violation of law or third-party rights.

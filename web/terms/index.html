2. What the service does

tailcat.app provides temporary one-to-one browser rooms and a Group Beta for up to 10 people including the host. Private rooms carry text, files, and voice notes and attempt WebRTC voice, video, or screen sharing. Group rooms carry group text, files, and voice notes, but do not offer live calls or screen sharing. The service provides no account, identity verification, contact list, offline file hosting, cloud chat history, backup, recovery, content moderation, or malware scanning.

A group host approves join requests, coordinates group text, and can remove members, pause joining, rotate the invitation, or close the room. Closing or refreshing the host page—or leaving a mobile host in the background for more than 120 seconds—ends the room for everyone, with no host transfer. New members receive no earlier messages; existing members can only attempt a 120-second recovery from the limited events still in the host's memory.

A person holding an invitation link or Tailcat address may connect or request admission. Nicknames are unverified and can be duplicated, and random member codes are not identity verification. Link encryption does not prove participant identity. Exchange invitations through a trusted channel and verify the people you intend to reach.

3. Beta infrastructure

Browser Tailcat traffic uses public DERP relays. Those relays are rate-limited, have no SLA or throughput target, and may change or stop at any time. Live media uses WebRTC and public STUN with no TURN fallback, so calls fail on some networks. We may change, suspend, or discontinue any part of the service for security, capacity, compliance, or maintenance.
